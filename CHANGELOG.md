# Changelog

## 22 Nov 2026
- Tell someone where I'm going: one tap sends a ready-made text with the walk, rough return time and a map link to where you are (inspired by AllTrails Lifeline / Live Share and Strava Beacon).
- Daylight check: each walk shows today's sunset and the latest time to head off, plus a coach heads-up when about 30 minutes of light are left (inspired by AllTrails and Gaia GPS sunset/daylight info).
- Punch combo call-outs: optional Easy, Mixed or Hard combos called out and shown big during boxing rounds (inspired by Precision Boxing Coach and Boxing Combo Timer).
